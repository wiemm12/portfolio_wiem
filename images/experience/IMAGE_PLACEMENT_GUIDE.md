## ✅ How to Add Your Images

### **Option 1: Manual Copy (Easiest)**
1. Find your two images on your computer
2. Rename them to:
   - `quetratech-system.jpg` (hardware photo)
   - `quetratech-app.jpg` (app screenshot)
3. Copy both files
4. Navigate to: `C:\Users\ASUS_USER\Documents\augment-projects\portfolio_wiem\images\experience\`
5. Paste the files into this folder
6. Refresh your browser (F5)
7. ✅ Images will appear in the QuetraTech experience card!

### **Option 2: Using VS Code**
1. In VS Code, look at the left sidebar
2. Expand the `images` folder
3. Expand the `experience` subfolder
4. Right-click on the `experience` folder
5. Select "Reveal in File Explorer"
6. Drag and drop your images into this folder
7. Rename them to the correct filenames
8. Refresh your browser (F5)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### **Images Not Showing?**

1. **Check the filename** - Must be EXACTLY:
   - `quetratech-system.jpg`
   - `quetratech-app.jpg`
   
2. **Check the location** - Must be in:
   - `images/experience/` folder
   
3. **Check the file extension** - Should be `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, or `.png`

4. **Refresh the browser** - Press F5 or Ctrl+R

5. **Clear cache** - Press Ctrl+Shift+R (hard refresh)

### **Images Look Stretched or Distorted?**

- Use images with 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio
- Recommended: 800x600 or 1200x900 pixels
